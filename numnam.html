💩 Poop Diagnostics
What your baby's output is telling you
Type What It Means NumNam Action
🪨 Type 1 — Hard pellets Constipation / Low fluid intake Add 1 tsp ghee or coconut oil to next purée. Offer 20ml extra water.
🌰 Type 2 — Lumpy Mild dehydration Offer sips of water after solids. Focus on high-moisture fruits (melon, pear).
🌭 Type 3 — Cracked Borderline normal Increase fluid-rich foods. Add cucumber or courgette to meals.
🍌 Type 4 — Smooth ✅ Optimal digestion! Great job! Your current fibre/fluid balance is perfect.
💛 Type 5 — Soft blobs Normal variation Monitor; likely fine. Check for any new foods introduced.
💦 Type 6 — Mushy Possible sensitivity or fibre spike Slow down on new high-fibre veggies. Monitor for allergies. Pause any new foods.
🍅 Red / Undigested Undigested fibre — common with carrots Mash food more thoroughly for 2 days. Normal if after red/orange foods.
💚 Green / Mucous Possible foremilk/hindmilk imbalance or teething Check milk balance. If persistent, consult your health visitor.
🧠 Smart Rescue Logic
If-this-then-that interventions
🪨 Dry/Hard Poop Rescue
"Ouch! Looks like baby is a bit backed up. Try adding 1 tsp of cold-pressed rapeseed or coconut oil to today's purée, or offer 20ml of extra water after the next solid meal."
💧 Hydration Alert
"Milk is down and solids are up — time for more fluids! Offer a few sips of water after every solid meal. In warm climates like India, hydration needs spike as milk reduces."
💚 Fibre Bridge
"Stool looks too loose. Try natural binders: banana, cooked rice, or plain yoghurt. Hold off on high-fibre veggies (prunes, peas) for the next 2 days."

⚠️ Safety Rules
Always keep these in mind
🚫

Avoid these under 12 months

Salt, added sugar, honey, whole nuts, raw hard vegetables (choking hazard), cow's milk as a main drink, undercooked eggs.

🥚

Introduce allergens early

Eggs, peanut butter (thinned), dairy, wheat, and fish — one at a time from 6 months. Watch for reactions for 3 days after each new allergen.

😮

Gagging vs choking

Gagging is a normal safety reflex — stay calm and let baby work it out. Only intervene if baby is silent and turning red/blue.

Know the difference — it will save you stress!
